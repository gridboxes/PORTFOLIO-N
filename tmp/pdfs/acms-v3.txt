1. Overview, Goals & Objectives

This project delivers two core enhancements to the existing Anantara Concorso Roma web platform: a Content
Management System (CMS) and a structured Car Submission Portal. These improvements will enable the
Anantara editorial team to manage website content independently and replace the current email-based car
submission process with a robust, trackable digital workflow.



The Anantara Concorso Roma is a prestigious Italian classic car concours event held in Rome, organized by
Anantara Hotels & Resorts. The platform serves both public visitors and internal administrators managing event
content, car submissions, volunteer registrations, and partner communications.



Goals

 Goal                        Description

 Editorial                   Empower the Anantara team to manage and update website content
 Independence                independently, reducing back-and-forth cycles and deployment dependencies.

 Structured Car              Replace the email-based submission process (which frequently results in
 Submissions                 missed applications due to spam filters) with a structured, trackable portal.

 Lightweight Access          Enable car owners to submit and track their applications without creating a full
 Control                     user account — using a personal tracking link for simplicity.

 Platform Extensibility      Establish shared patterns that can be reused across volunteer registration,
                             Cars & Coffee, and future event modules.

6. Implementation Roadmap

The project is structured across four phases to prioritise foundational infrastructure, then submission portals, then
ancillary features. A staggered approach allows UAT on stable modules before new ones are introduced.




Phase 1: CMS Platform (Week 1 – 8)

Kick-off and environment setup run in the first two weeks, followed immediately by CMS development. The goal is
full editorial independence for the Anantara team.



 Scope                        Content editor, image & gallery management, news & page management,
                              role-based access control.

 Mechanism                    A CMS interface integrated into the existing website — editors can publish,
                              update, and schedule content without touching code.

 Objective                    Anantara manages and publishes website content entirely on their own — no
                              Mtel involvement needed.


Design Confirmation Milestone:

    -​   28 Jun: CMS Part 1

Phase 2: Submission & Registration Portal (Week 1 – 8)

Runs in parallel with Phase 1. Replaces email-based submissions with a structured, trackable portal — extended
to cover Volunteer and Cars & Coffee registrations on the same framework.



 Scope                      Public submission form, token-based tracking, admin dashboard,
                            approval-gated access, Volunteer & Cars & Coffee registration.

 Mechanism                  Account-free submission with a magic link for status tracking. Staff manage
                            everything from one dashboard.

 Objective                  Every submission is captured, trackable, and manageable — no more lost
