01 Background & Objectives
 FleetCo operates a fleet of 80 vehicles, each rented out with a dedicated driver. Our anchor client, Thailand Post,
 needs a self-service platform to request vehicles on demand — for ad hoc daily deliveries, multi-day or multi-week
 rentals, and long-term engagements spanning multiple months. Today this is handled manually over calls, chat,
 and spreadsheets, which does not scale and gives neither side real-time visibility.

 We are commissioning a two-sided B2B web platform: an operations portal for FleetCo and a self-service portal
 for clients. Thailand Post is the launch client, but the platform must be multi-client from day one — FleetCo will
 onboard additional enterprise accounts on the same system with full account/vendor management.


 Objectives
  ■ Let clients request, extend, and manage vehicle-with-driver rentals end to end without phone coordination.

  ■ Give FleetCo a single operational view: fleet inventory, driver assignment, live vehicle tracking, utilization, and
    revenue.

  ■ Digitize the full commercial document chain — quotation → invoice → payment confirmation → tax invoice —
    inside the platform.

  ■ Support multiple client organizations (multi-tenant) with clean data separation, per-client pricing, and per-client
    reporting.


 Success measures (directional)
  ■ 100% of Thailand Post vehicle requests placed through the platform within 3 months of launch.

  ■ Quotation issued within minutes of a request, not hours; zero documents produced outside the system.

  ■ Fleet utilization visible in real time; month-end revenue reporting available without manual reconciliation.

03 The Rental Model
 Every rental = one vehicle + one dedicated driver, booked as a single unit. The platform must support four rental
 patterns with the same underlying flow:

 Rental type             Duration           Typical use                     Design implications

 Ad hoc / Daily          Single day         Daily delivery runs, overflow   Fast request flow; same-day or next-day availability
                                            demand                          check; quick re-book of a previous request.

 Short term              Multiple days      Campaign or peak-period         Date-range picker; per-day pricing; partial-period
                                            support                         modification.

 Medium term             Weeks              Seasonal capacity               Weekly rate logic; extension flow before end date.

 Long term               Multiple           Dedicated routes, embedded      Monthly rate; recurring monthly invoicing; renewal
                         months             capacity                        reminders; mid-term vehicle/driver replacement
                                                                            handling.


  ■ Extensions and early termination: an active rental can be extended (subject to availability) or ended early;
    both trigger a revised quotation/commercial confirmation.

  ■ Replacement: if a vehicle breaks down or a driver is unavailable, FleetCo swaps the unit; the client sees
    continuity, the ledger records the change.

  ■ Booking states (proposed): Requested → Quoted → Accepted → Assigned → Active → Completed →
    Invoiced → Paid → Closed (plus Declined / Canceled branches). Design the status vocabulary and color system
    once, reuse everywhere.

04 FleetCo Portal — Feature Requirements

 4.1 Operations dashboard
  ■ At-a-glance: vehicles available / on rental / in maintenance, pending requests, quotations awaiting acceptance,
    invoices awaiting payment, today's active rentals.

  ■ Alert strip for exceptions: expiring rentals, overdue invoices, document expiries, unassigned bookings.

 4.2 Vehicle inventory management (CRUD)
  ■ Vehicle records: plate number, type/class (e.g., 4-wheel truck, van, pickup), capacity/payload, photos, home
    depot, purchase/lease info, current status.

  ■ Status lifecycle: Available, Reserved, On Rental, In Maintenance, Out of Service — with status history.

  ■ Compliance documents with expiry alerts: registration, insurance (compulsory + voluntary), tax sticker,
    inspection.

  ■ Maintenance log: scheduled service, repairs, cost history, odometer readings; block availability during
    maintenance windows.


 4.3 Driver management & assignment
  ■ Driver roster: profile, license class and expiry, contact, employment status, assigned home vehicle (if any),
    leave calendar.

  ■ Assignment engine: pair vehicle + driver to a booking; detect conflicts (double booking, driver on leave, license
    mismatch with vehicle class).

  ■ Reassignment / swap flow mid-rental with automatic client notification.

 4.4 Booking & schedule management
  ■ Request inbox: all incoming client requests with status, SLA timer, and one-click path to quotation.

  ■ Fleet calendar / Gantt view: every vehicle as a row, rentals as bars across time — the core planning surface for
    multi-week and multi-month rentals.

  ■ Manual booking creation on behalf of a client (phone/email orders still happen).

 4.5 Client account / vendor management (multi-client CRUD)
  ■ Client organization records: company profile, tax ID and registered address (needed for tax invoices),
    branch/site list, billing terms, credit terms.

  ■ Per-client rate cards: pricing by vehicle type × rental duration tier; effective dates; discount rules.

  ■ Client user management: invite users, assign client-side roles, deactivate.

  ■ Contract/agreement repository per client (upload signed MSAs, rate agreements).

  ■ Data isolation: a client can only ever see its own bookings, documents, and pricing.

05 Client Portal (Thailand Post) — Feature Requirements

 5.1 Vehicle request flow (the hero flow)
  ■ Request on demand: choose vehicle type and quantity, rental type (daily / days / weeks / multi-month), start
    date, duration or end date, pickup/reporting location, and job notes.

  ■ Availability signal before submitting: show whether the requested type/dates look available, tentative, or
    unavailable, so expectations are set early.

  ■ Multi-vehicle requests in one submission (e.g., 5 vans for 2 weeks) treated as one commercial case.

  ■ Repeat / re-book: duplicate a past request with new dates in two clicks — daily-delivery clients will live in this.

 5.2 Rental management
  ■ My rentals: active, upcoming, and past rentals with status, assigned vehicle and driver details (name, phone,
    plate number).

  ■ Extend, modify, or cancel a rental — with clear rules on what triggers a revised quotation.

  ■ Live tracking of vehicles currently on rental to Thailand Post (their vehicles only).

  ■ Issue reporting: flag a problem on a rental (late arrival, breakdown, driver issue) that lands in FleetCo's
    operations inbox.


 5.3 Commercial documents
  ■ Quotation inbox: notification when FleetCo issues a quotation; view online, download PDF, Accept or Decline
    with comment.

  ■ Invoice inbox: view/download invoices, see amount due and due date, mark as paid with payment date,
    reference number, and optional payment slip upload.

  ■ Tax invoice inbox: download tax invoices once issued by FleetCo.

  ■ Billing history: full document trail per booking and per month.

 5.4 Client dashboard & reporting
  ■ Overview: active rentals count, upcoming starts/ends, pending quotations, unpaid invoices, monthly spend to
    date.

  ■ Usage reports: rentals and spend by month, by vehicle type, by requesting branch/department.

  ■ Multi-user with internal approval (optional per client): a requester submits, a manager approves before it
    reaches FleetCo — design as a toggleable step.

06 Quotation → Invoice → Tax Invoice Chain
 This is a first-class module, not an afterthought. The three documents form one linked chain per booking, with
 notifications passing between the two sides at each step. No document should ever be created outside the
 platform.


 6.1 Document flow & notification chain

 Step      Actor             Action                                                       Counterparty notification

 1         Client            Submits vehicle request                                      FleetCo operations notified

 2         FleetCo           Issues Quotation from the platform (linked to the request)   Client notified; quotation appears in
                                                                                          client portal

 3         Client            Accepts quotation (or declines with comment)                 FleetCo notified; booking moves to
                                                                                          Accepted

 4         FleetCo           Assigns vehicle + driver; rental runs                        Client sees assignment details

 5         FleetCo           Issues Invoice (one-off, or monthly for long-term rentals)   Client notified; invoice appears with
                                                                                          due date

 6         Client            Pays manually (bank transfer) and marks as paid with         FleetCo finance notified to verify
                             reference/slip

 7         FleetCo           Verifies payment, issues Tax Invoice / Receipt               Client notified; tax invoice available for
                                                                                          download


  ■ Every document carries a link back to its booking and its predecessor (tax invoice → invoice → quotation).

  ■ FleetCo can revise a quotation (v2, v3...) before acceptance; superseded versions stay visible but clearly
     marked.

  ■ Payment verification step: 'marked as paid' by the client is a claim, not a settlement — FleetCo finance
     confirms before the tax invoice is released. Design both the happy path and the mismatch path (amount short,
     wrong reference).

 6.2 The document editor — split-screen WYSIWYG (key design requirement)
 All three documents share one editor pattern: a single screen, form on the left, live A4 preview on the right.
 What you type is what you get — the preview is the actual rendered document, pixel-faithful to the final PDF.

  ■ Left panel (input): structured fields — client (auto-filled from account record), booking reference, line items
    (description, vehicle type, quantity, unit, unit price, amount), discounts, VAT, remarks, payment terms, validity
    period.

  ■ Right panel (preview): true A4 page rendered live as the user types, including FleetCo letterhead, document
    number, and totals. Multi-page documents paginate in the preview.

  ■ Line items auto-calculate: quantity × unit price, subtotal, discount, VAT 7%, withholding tax display where
    applicable, grand total — in THB with Thai Baht text line.

  ■ Actions: Save draft, Preview PDF, Issue (locks the document, generates the number, fires the notification),
    Duplicate, Convert (quotation → invoice pre-filled).

  ■ Issued documents are immutable; corrections happen via credit note / revised version, never by editing history.

 6.3 Thai tax compliance requirements (must-have on the tax invoice)
  ■ Sequential, gap-free document numbering per document type (separate series for quotation / invoice / tax
    invoice).

  ■ Seller and buyer legal name, registered address, 13-digit tax ID, and head office / branch number.

  ■ The words “Tax Invoice” displayed in both Thai and English, issue date, and VAT amount shown separately at
    7%.

  ■ Thai + English bilingual template support; amount-in-words in Thai.

  ■ Note to design: the A4 templates for all three documents need visual design as part of this engagement —
    treat them as branded artifacts clients will judge us by.

07 Vehicle Financing & Fleet Expansion (FleetCo Internal)
 The current fleet of 80 vehicles is fixed and may not be enough to serve growing B2B demand. FleetCo will expand
 the fleet by financing additional vehicles through bank loans / hire-purchase. The platform needs an internal
 module where every financed vehicle carries its full cost structure, and — once that vehicle is rented out — shows
 whether the rental revenue covers the financing cost, what the monthly margin is, and how long until the vehicle
 pays for itself (ROI).

 Visibility: this module is strictly FleetCo-internal (finance and management roles only). No cost, margin, or
 financing data is ever exposed on the client side.


 7.1 Financing record per vehicle (CRUD)
  ■ Acquisition cost structure: base vehicle cost, down payment, financed amount, interest rate and total interest
    over the term, insurance cost (annual premium and monthly equivalent), registration and one-time fees.

  ■ Loan terms: lender/bank, contract number, term length, monthly installment, start date, first/last payment
    dates.

  ■ Payment progress: installments paid vs. total (e.g., 14 of 48), months remaining to finish payment,
    outstanding principal, next payment date — shown as a progress bar plus figures.

  ■ Total cost of ownership view: base cost + total interest + insurance over the term = all-in cost, the
    denominator for ROI.

  ■ Payment schedule table per vehicle (due date, installment, principal/interest split, status) with the ability to
    record early settlement.


 7.2 Rental coverage & margin (per financed vehicle)
  ■ Monthly cost line: installment + monthly insurance equivalent (+ optional maintenance allocation) = monthly
    carrying cost.

  ■ Monthly revenue line: actual rental revenue generated by this vehicle this month, pulled automatically from its
    bookings.

  ■ Margin: revenue − carrying cost, in THB and %, per month and cumulative since acquisition.

  ■ Coverage indicator: a clear covered / not-covered signal — does this month’s rental revenue cover this
    month’s financing cost? Coverage ratio (e.g., 1.4×) with a status color.

  ■ Idle burn alert: a financed vehicle sitting idle still costs its installment — flag financed vehicles with low
    utilization prominently.


 7.3 ROI & break-even tracking
  ■ Break-even / ROI countdown: cumulative net margin vs. all-in cost — how many months until the vehicle has
    fully paid for itself, based on its actual average monthly margin. Show projected ROI date.

  ■ ROI progress visual per vehicle (e.g., 62% recovered, ~11 months to break-even at current run rate).

  ■ Scenario sensitivity: recalculate ROI timeline when the rental rate or utilization assumption changes (slider or
    editable assumption fields).

 7.4 Portfolio dashboard & acquisition simulator
  ■ Financing portfolio view: all financed vehicles in one table — outstanding balance, months remaining,
    monthly obligation total, portfolio coverage ratio, vehicles at risk (uncovered or idle).

  ■ Total monthly obligation vs. total contracted rental revenue from long-term B2B rentals — the headline
    solvency view for management.

  ■ Acquisition simulator (pre-purchase): before committing to the bank, model a candidate vehicle — enter
    base cost, financing terms, insurance, and expected B2B rental rate/duration — and see projected monthly
    margin, coverage, and ROI timeline. Turns fleet expansion into a data-backed decision.

  ■ Exports: portfolio and per-vehicle financing reports to Excel/PDF for bank and shareholder discussions.

 7.5 Design implications
  ■ Two key surfaces: (a) the portfolio table with dense financial columns and status colors, (b) the per-vehicle
    financing detail page combining payment progress, margin trend chart, and ROI countdown in one narrative.

  ■ Numbers must be scannable: THB formatting with thousands separators, negative margins visually
    unmistakable, and consistent month-based language (always “months” written in full).

08 Platform Principles & Non-Functional Requirements
  ■ B2B throughout, never B2C: this is an enterprise contract platform — organization accounts, negotiated rate
    cards, quotations and credit terms, approval workflows, and formal tax documents. No consumer patterns: no
    public marketplace, no self-signup, no ratings/reviews, no retail-style checkout. Every screen should read as a
    professional operations tool.

  ■ Multi-tenant by design: every entity (booking, document, vehicle assignment, report) is scoped to a client
    account; onboarding client #2 must require zero structural change.

  ■ Scalability: assume fleet growth beyond 80 vehicles and 10+ client organizations without redesign; lists, filters,
    and search must handle volume.

  ■ Web-first, responsive: desktop-optimized for FleetCo operations and finance; client portal must be
    comfortable on tablet and usable on mobile for approvals and mark-as-paid.

  ■ Language & locale: Thai and English UI toggle; THB currency throughout; Buddhist Era / Gregorian date
    display consideration for documents; weeks start Monday.

  ■ Notifications: in-app notification center + email for every step of the commercial chain; design a consistent
    notification anatomy.

  ■ Permissions & audit: role-based access on both sides; immutable audit trail on financial documents and
    payment status changes.

  ■ Reliability of tracking: map views degrade gracefully when GPS signal is stale — always show last-known
    position with timestamp.
