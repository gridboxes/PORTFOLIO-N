Overview

Everything ready before you land.

ThaiPass consolidates the essential travel services an international visitor needs — built as a travel companion, not just a booking tool. The guiding idea throughout: arrive prepared, not overwhelmed.

Voucher PackagesFastPass TransporteSIMInsurance
Problem

A fragmented, stressful journey.

Travelling to Thailand means juggling multiple platforms before and after arrival — each with its own friction.

  • Confusing pre-arrival requirements (e.g. the TDAC process)
  • Long queues at airport immigration
  • Difficulty getting internet access on arrival
  • Uncertainty around transportation options
  • No single trusted source — managing many platforms at once

How might we design a single, trusted platform that simplifies the entire journey — from pre-arrival preparation to arrival and beyond?

Research

How I approached it.

Four research lenses framed the work before any screen was drawn:

Traveler journey mapping Airport friction points Trust & reliability concerns Mobile-first behaviour

What I found.

01Prepare before landing. Travelers want everything sorted before the plane doors open.
02Time is the value. Saving time at the airport and setup beat every other benefit.
03Trust is non-negotiable. Users strongly prefer verified providers and official processes.
04Bundling converts. Grouping services raised both convenience and conversion.
Process

From strategy to screens.

1Product strategyFramed ThaiPass as a travel companion — “everything ready before you land.”
2Feature structuringOrganised around 5 core services: Vouchers, FastPass, Transport, eSIM, Insurance.
3User-flow designSimplified the journey into 3 phases: Purchase → Arrival → Support.
4Trust & clarityDirect links to official TDAC, transparent pricing, verified vendors only.
Final Design

Five services, one guided journey.

Each service was designed to disappear into the trip — minimal decisions, clear trust signals, everything retrievable the moment it’s needed.
