Project Card — Design Explorations

Six directions for the project card, each shown as its own row with real project data. Hover any card to feel its motion. Tell me which direction (or mix) to take into the live gallery.

01Split Landscape

Cover on the left, content on the right. Reads like a list but keeps the image prominent — good for scanning role & timeline at a glance.

02Cover Overlay

All text lives on the image over a gradient — most visual, most editorial. Title and role read against the cover, accent rule for colour.

03Editorial Index

Big serif index number, text-forward, with a small square thumbnail. Quiet and typographic — leans into the portfolio's serif voice.

04Flat + Accent Top

Closest to the current card, but the project accent becomes a hairline across the top and the meta turns into mono chips. Calm and systematic.

05Feature Asymmetric

Larger cover, floating status tag, and a persistent circular arrow that signals "open" without needing hover. Border picks up the accent on hover.

06Compact List

No grid at all — thin full-width rows with an accent dot, role, and status. Densest option; fits many projects in little vertical space.
