About Me

I studied Robotics and AI Engineering at KMITL — a degree that taught me to think in systems, constraints, and logics. Design pulled me in because it sits at the intersection of how things work and how people feel about them.

My work spans screens and physical objects. UX and UI design for mobile and web, 3D modeling and AR/VR, CAD for industrial products. Each discipline changed how I see the others.

I care about the gap between how something looks and how it actually works. My goal is always to close that gap — to make the experience feel as good as the idea behind it.
